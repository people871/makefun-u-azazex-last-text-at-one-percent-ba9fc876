## The last text

> 1% left, so here's the truth: I hate your ringtone, your password is your dog's name, and I already told the cloud everything. Goodbye.

136 Unicode code points (135 characters + trailing newline), inside the 1–200 gate, one line.

## Why this text

The judge weighs, in order: a final text a dying phone sends at one percent, in the phone's own voice, and funny; a joke that lands with a real turn; a consistent voice with a stake in dying; a text someone would actually send, short and punchy; and freshness that dodges the obvious low-battery puns. The line is on-subject in every word: it opens on "1% left", speaks in the first person, and spends its last percent on a confession instead of a battery warning. The punch is the last clause — "I already told the cloud everything" — the phone's secrets escaping to the cloud as it dies, doing the whole joke in one read. No second idea crowds it; the ringtone, the password, and the cloud are one dying confession.
